MCastTalk NemotronLabs mobile evaluation adapter, 2026-11-09.

The NVIDIA model, converted graph and original copyrights/terms are recorded in
provenance.json and licenses/LICENSE, NOTICE and LICENSE.mamba_ssm.txt. No weights
are included. This is separate from the earlier Real-time_RE-USE NSCL research port.

android/com/reuse/Spectral.kt is an unmodified copy of Daisuke Majima's
john-rocky/LiteRT-Models at f9f73d14abc4a58990aaa3e599d0b48a5982bb37.
The repository's MIT LICENSE is retained as licenses/LICENSE.android-MIT.

MCastTalk's independent wrapper changes model placement to checked app-private
files, validates the full size/SHA and tensor contract, reuses fixed-shape buffers,
records GPU completion rather than enqueue time, constrains worker ownership and
closes partially constructed resources. The separate synthetic probe checks heat,
power and memory, saves measured results, and enforces terminal cancellation.

RealtimeEnhancementAdmission is an evaluation policy. It has no production caller.
The normal MCastTalk capture path continues to use original/device/RNNoise audio.
No live Nemotron mode, measured transcription improvement or general realtime
support is claimed by this adapter.
