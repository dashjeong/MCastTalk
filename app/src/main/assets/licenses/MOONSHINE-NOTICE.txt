GuideCast integration note
--------------------------
The application downloads and invokes the Korean legacy non-streaming TINY
speech-to-text model through Moonshine Voice 0.1.5. GuideCast does not modify
the model weights. Application-side changes are limited to Android lifecycle,
download verification, audio chunking, recovery, and local broadcast routing.
